NextFund came out of a gap we kept hitting when evaluating financial agents: benchmarks report a final portfolio return, but give no way to see the reasoning, analyst judgments, or trades that got the agent there. We built NextFund as a platform that keeps that whole decision path - from market observation to executed trade - inspectable, backed by consistent live market access and coordinated multi-agent analysis.

Its centerpiece is an interactive Trading Arena, where you can compare models across markets, inspect equity curves, and drill from a leaderboard result down to the individual justification behind it. We demonstrated it on Hong Kong, U.S., and China A-share equities.
